## 0.1.0 — 2026-09-28

First release.

- Theme on top of Bootstrap 5.3 with tokens, six accent colors and restyled core components
- Textmode utilities: frames, dot leaders, key-value lists, hexdump lines, block logos, photo and avatar placeholders, log entries, timeline, cursor, text spinner
- Examples: component page and personal blog
